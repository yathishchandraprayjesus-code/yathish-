# Awesome Seedream 5 Prompts, AI Image Workflows and Commercial Visual Prompt Templates

A practical, SEO-friendly resource for **Seedream 5 prompts**, **Seedream prompts**, **Seedream 5 image prompts**, **AI image generation prompts**, **product photography prompts**, **AI ad creative prompts**, and **source-frame prompts for image-to-video**.

## Core Prompt Formula

\`\`\`text
Create a [style / medium] image of [subject].
Composition: [framing, crop, angle, layout].
Environment: [background or scene].
Lighting: [type, direction, mood].
Details: [textures, colors, focus points].
Purpose: [ad creative, product page, social post, poster, concept art, source frame].
Mood: [emotion or brand feel].
Restrictions: [what to avoid].
\`\`\`

## Commercial Image Prompt Structure

1. Subject: Hero product or focal point.
2. Composition: Angle, framing, negative space.
3. Environment: Setting, surface, backdrop.
4. Lighting: Softness, direction, color temperature.
5. Details: Texture, reflections, material physics.
6. Intended Use: E-commerce hero, banner, paid ad.
7. Restrictions: Negative boundaries (no distorted text, extra items, watermarks).

## Image Editing Formula

\`\`\`text
Edit the image to [specific change].
Preserve [what must stay the same].
Improve [what should become better].
Style: [desired style].
Restrictions: [what to avoid].
\`\`\`

## Source Frames for Image-to-Video

- Clear single focal point
- Visible atmospheric and spatial depth
- Clean separation between foreground and background
- Stable perspective and room for subtle motion
- Zero reliance on tiny readable text
